Glossary

AFS
Annual Financial Statements: the authority's full set of year-end statements and notes.
FDS
Financial Data Schedule: a standardized schedule of financial data, by program, submitted to HUD.
COCC
Central Office Cost Center: the accounting unit for central overhead and fee income.
PHA
Public housing authority, also called a housing agency.
REAC
HUD's Real Estate Assessment Center, which receives financial submissions.
IPA
Independent public accountant: the outside auditor.
Net position
What an organization owns minus what it owes.
Fund accounting
Tracking money separately by program or purpose so restricted funds are used as intended.
