Lesson 9: AFS and FDS preparation and data entry

1. The big picture

The AFS (Annual Financial Statements) are the authority's full year-end statements and notes, audited by an independent accountant. The FDS (Financial Data Schedule) is a standardized schedule of the same financial data, broken out by program, that is submitted electronically to HUD through the Financial Assessment Subsystem for Public Housing (FASS-PH). The audited FDS also appears as supplementary information in the audit.

An unaudited FDS is due within two months of the fiscal year end. The audited submission is due within nine months. Common fiscal year ends work out like this:

Fiscal year endUnaudited dueAudited due
December 31February 28September 30
March 31May 31December 31
June 30August 31March 31
September 30November 30June 30

Late submissions can lead to audit findings, and for voucher programs HUD may withhold or reduce administrative fees.

2. Building the AFS

  • Statement of net position: assets, liabilities, and the three parts of net position (Lesson 2)
  • Statement of revenues, expenses, and changes in net position: the year's results
  • Statement of cash flows: where cash came from and went
  • Notes: accounting policies and details behind the numbers

Start from the final trial balance. Each account rolls into one statement line, so a clean chart of accounts (Lesson 3) makes this step quick.

3. Understanding the FDS layout

The FDS has one column for each public housing project, the COCC, the voucher programs, and other business activities, plus columns that eliminate internal amounts and show totals. Rows are numbered balance sheet, revenue, and expense lines. You also choose an asset management type, which decides how the COCC and eliminations are reported, so pick the one that matches how the authority actually keeps its books.

4. Mapping accounts to FDS lines

Mapping means assigning each account in your chart of accounts to one FDS line. Line names below are paraphrased, so use HUD's current FDS line list for the exact lines.

5. Data entry walkthrough

  1. Sign in to HUD's secure systems with an ID that has FASS-PH access for your authority.
  2. Create a new submission for the fiscal year and choose the type: unaudited or audited.
  3. Enter the data collection information and the FDS data. You can enter programs in any order, and HUD has offered a spreadsheet upload tool, so check what is currently available.
  4. Run the system's validation checks and fix every error and warning.
  5. Submit. For an audited submission, the independent accountant must first mark it "IPA Agree."
  6. Check the status page to confirm the submission was received and scored.

Screens and tools change, so use HUD's current FASS-PH user guide alongside this lesson.

6. Checks before you submit

  • FDS totals agree to the AFS, including each net position component.
  • Interprogram due to and due from balances offset, and internal fees are eliminated.
  • Cash matches the bank reconciliations.
  • Each program's revenue and expense lines are reasonable compared with last year and with the budget.
  • Restricted amounts, such as voucher funds, agree to HUD's records.
  • Every account maps to exactly one FDS line.

7. After submission

  • HUD scores the financial submission, and you can review the score. Read any HUD review comments and respond by the stated date.
  • If the audited figures differ from the unaudited ones, HUD uses the audited data, so reconcile the differences and explain them.
  • File the audited package with the Federal Audit Clearinghouse on time.
  • Keep support for every line for future audits.

Practice: The FDS shows a $1,000 due from the COCC in a project column, but the COCC column shows no matching due to. What has gone wrong, and where do you look first?

HUD's systems, line items, and deadlines change. Always confirm current requirements on HUD's official pages before submitting.
