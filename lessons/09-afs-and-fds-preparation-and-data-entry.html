Lesson 9: AFS and FDS preparation and data entry

  1. The big picture: what the AFS and FDS are, how they relate, unaudited versus audited submissions, and due dates
  2. Building the AFS: statement of net position, statement of revenues, expenses and changes in net position, statement of cash flows, and notes
  3. Understanding the FDS layout: line items and program columns
  4. Mapping accounts to FDS lines: a worked crosswalk from a sample chart of accounts
  5. Data entry walkthrough: entering FDS data in HUD's online submission system, common validation errors, and fixes
  6. Checks before you submit: tie-outs between the AFS and FDS, program balances, and interprogram due to/from amounts
  7. After submission: HUD review comments, resubmissions, and reconciling audited figures to the unaudited submission

HUD's systems, line items, and deadlines change. Always confirm current requirements on HUD's official pages before submitting.
