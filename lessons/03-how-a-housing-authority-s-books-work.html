Core documents for the annual funding process

Each program and funding source has its own yearly paperwork. The authority's accounting records are the source for most of these documents, which is why the chart of accounts, the AFS, and the FDS matter so much.

Program / funding sourceCore annual documentsSubmitted to
Public Housing: Operating Fund (HUD)Form HUD-52722 (Utilities Expense Level) and Form HUD-52723 (Total Program Expense Level) for each project, plus the board-approved operating budget. HUD funds operating subsidy on a calendar-year basis and issues an annual notice with deadlines.HUD, through the Public Housing Portal
Public Housing: Capital Fund (HUD)PHA 5-Year and Annual Plan; Form HUD-50075.1 (Annual Statement/Performance and Evaluation Report) for each open grant; Form HUD-50075.2 (Five-Year Action Plan); the grant agreement and ACC amendment once the award is known.HUD
Housing Choice Voucher (HUD)HUD's annual HCV funding notice and your funding letter (HAP renewal funding and administrative fees); monthly VMS leasing and cost data, which drives the next year's renewal funding; the year-end financial submission (FDS).HUD
Mainstream Voucher (HUD)Same documents as the HCV program: renewal funding is included with HCV funding, but leasing and other data are reported separately.HUD
Project Based Vouchers (HUD)Paid from the authority's HCV funding, so the HCV documents apply, plus the HAP contract with each owner for the project-based units.HUD and the property owner
PBRA and New Construction / Substantial Rehabilitation (HUD Multifamily)HAP contract and annual funding renewal; annual rent adjustment request (OCAF, AAF, or budget-based) with the rent schedule (Form HUD-92458); monthly HAP voucher through TRACS; annual owner financial statements.HUD or its contract administrator
Rental Assistance Demonstration (HUD)RAD conversion commitment, RAD Use Agreement, and the new HAP contract. After conversion, the annual documents follow the PBV or PBRA process.HUD
Multifamily Housing (HUD)Program-specific agreements and rent adjustments, plus annual financial statements (generally audited) submitted to HUD.HUD
Tax Credit (IRS)State allocation and extended use agreement; IRS Form 8609 (credit allocation and certification); annual owner compliance certification to the state agency. Often used together with the HUD programs above, so a property may need both sets of documents.State housing finance agency and IRS

Forms, systems, and deadlines change each year. HUD publishes annual notices (for example, the operating subsidy and HCV funding notices), so confirm the current documents and due dates on HUD's website or with your HUD field office.
